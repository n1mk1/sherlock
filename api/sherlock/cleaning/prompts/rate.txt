Levels, most severe first:
- breaks: answers or models would be wrong (numbers stored as text in the outcome or a measure).
- skews: answers would be biased (placeholders such as -999 pulling an average; impossible values; repeated records inflating counts).
- splits: groups would be split or mislabeled ("<value>" and "<Value> " showing as two groups; near-duplicate categories).
- check: probably real, worth a look (an extreme but plausible value; missing values with no rule to recover them).
- cosmetic: changes no answer (spacing or case in a free-text column).

For each finding give its ref exactly as written, a level, one sentence on what it does to an analysis, and likely_real: true when the values look real rather than errors. Each finding is one line: ref · what the check finds · column · the column's role and type · rows affected and their share · up to five examples · the planned fix · and, when known, "meaning:" and what the column records.
